What does a capital budget buy in risk reduction?

An entire public transformer register, explicit assumptions and uncertainty you can inspect.

Partial research result

Age-only and health-index evaluation and replacement rules are unavailable because the source has no age, installation date or measured condition inputs. Selected published CNAIM equations are implemented and tested, but there is no calibrated CNAIM fleet result. Annual burn labels do not establish condition-based failure mechanisms. Feature timestamps and asset identities are undocumented. The evaluation is retrospective, with disjoint observed predictor profiles; it is not a verified prospective failure forecast. Costs and intervention effects are scenario assumptions, so the frontier is a research result on a public register, not asset-management advice. This is a research result on a public register from Cauca, Colombia; it is not engineering, investment or asset-management advice, and it has not been validated on any Canadian utility's data.

A register audit changes the interpretation

75 excess identical rows are retained because physical identities are absent. 5395 length values exceed an assumed 100 km plausibility threshold. Length is excluded; its ranking impact is measured separately. All records lack age and measured condition inputs.
